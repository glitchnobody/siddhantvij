    Why use this stack?

    I wanted something simple and easy to maintain. The site runs on Cloudflare and loads super quick. I believe that even with AI there is no point in using bloat in software. Keeping it minimal both on design and code.

    Any inspiration for this site?

    I have always loved stippling art, and while browsing for inspo I came across Noel’s website. I loved how he separated the complex part of the canvas as the header and footer while seamlessly blending the central content that remains clean and to the point.

    From there it was just a matter of choosing a theme, and space cats is basically the first thing that came to my mind. Stars are already like stippling art in the sky, and I love cats :P

    What is the cost of running this site?

    $0.00, apart from the .com domain. A free Cloudflare account and a free Resend account is all that was needed.
